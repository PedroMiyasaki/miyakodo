Today marks a big moment for DuckLabs: we are joining Amazon Web Services (AWS). We expect the move to be completed by early September. When Mark Raasveldt and I started DuckLabs five years ago, we wanted to create a lasting home for the team behind DuckDB. Since then, that team has grown to more than 30 people, and DuckDB has become more widely used than we could ever have imagined. 

Joining AWS gives us the resources and reach to bring DuckDB, DuckLake and the Quack protocol to many more developers and organisations. It will also allow us to pursue ideas on a scale that would have been difficult for us to reach alone. Our team will remain together in Amsterdam, working for the Duck Stack community as an AWS subsidiary. 

Just as importantly, the foundations of the project will remain in place. DuckDB and the other open-source components of the Duck Stack will remain free and open source under the MIT licence, with the non-profit DuckDB Foundation continuing to steward the projects. I am immensely proud of what our team and community have built together. This is the end of one chapter for DuckLabs, but also the beginning of one that we believe will take DuckDB much further.
