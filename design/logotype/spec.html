Logotype · 01
The mark
The name knocked out of an inked block. The block is not a container the wordmark sits in - block and letterforms are roughened as one impression, which is why the edge chews and the counters stay open.
Clear space = 0.30em - the block's own top padding 0.30em
Pedro Miyasaki
Construction
Block padding · .30em top · .38em sides · .34em bottom
Wordmark · Bricolage Grotesque 800, uppercase, -.03em tracking
Impression · feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap on the whole block
Clear space
Nothing enters 0.30em on any side - the same value as the block's
top padding, so the mark never sits tighter to its neighbours than
the wordmark sits to its own edge. Measured in em of the wordmark.

Roughness is a function of size
The displacement is measured in absolute pixels, so a single filter cannot serve every size - scale="7" reads as ink at 900px and as mud at 120px. Three bands, plus a clean cut below 120px. Never invent a fourth.

Minimum sizes
Stamp wordmark · 96px wide. Below that it stops being readable and the badge takes over.
Badge · 24px. That is the favicon floor.
Baked, not live
The impression is applied at render size and exported. Never scale a rendered PNG of the mark - re-render it at the target size so the
roughness band is right.

Five ways to break it
Each of these has a specific failure behind it, not a preference.
Pedro Miyasaki

Pedro Miyasaki
