What “human in the loop” actually costs

At forty thousand documents a month, a two-minute review is thirteen hundred hours a year. That is not a workflow step. That is most of a full-time hire, hidden inside a slide that said “human in the loop” and moved on.

The number nobody asks for is the review rate - what fraction actually gets looked at. When it turns out to be four per cent, the loop was never the safeguard anyone bought.
